# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and kind in technical disagreement.
- Assume good intent while still examining evidence critically.
- Respect privacy, consent, licensing, and creative ownership.
- Give credit and accept correction.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately enabling non-consensual voice imitation or abusive use.
- Repeated disruption, bad-faith argument, or retaliation after moderation.
- Using project spaces to promote illegal or rights-infringing activity.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact method maintained in the
repository's security or community settings. Maintainers will acknowledge the
report, protect confidentiality where possible, investigate proportionately,
and may warn, restrict, suspend, or permanently exclude participants.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these rules.
They must apply them consistently and disclose conflicts of interest.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public.
It is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
